Frequently asked questions

Can’t find your answer? Send us a message.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope: the number of pages, features, integrations and how much content we need to create. After a short conversation we send a written quote that lists exactly what is included. You can use the budget field in the contact form to tell us your range.

How long will my project take?

A small website can often be ready in a few weeks, while custom applications and school systems take longer. We give a realistic timeline in the quote, and the biggest factor is usually how quickly content and feedback arrive.

Do you provide hosting?

We can set up hosting for you or deploy to the provider you already use. We will recommend a setup that fits your traffic and budget, and explain what the ongoing hosting costs will be before you commit.

Do I need to buy a domain name?

Yes, you need a domain such as yourbusiness.com. We can advise on choosing one and connect it to your site. We recommend registering the domain in your own name so you stay in control of it.

What happens after the site is live?

We hand over the project with documentation and answer launch-week questions. For ongoing updates, backups, security patches and fixes, you can add a maintenance plan, or contact us only when you need help.

How is the project delivered?

You see progress along the way and review the work before launch. At delivery you receive the finished site or application, access to what was built and the documentation needed to run it. The details are agreed in your written quote.

Can I request changes during the project?

Yes. Reasonable adjustments are part of the design and review stages. If a request changes the agreed scope, we tell you how it affects time and cost before going ahead.

Do you work with schools and individuals?

Yes. We work with businesses, schools, startups and individuals, and we adapt scope and communication to fit your team’s size and experience.
